## Confirmed source gaps
BIZ-001 High / confirmed defect: financial payment and receipt retry safety absent. invoices.service.js:290-298 and :361-369 accept only amountCents/notes and run each call as a fresh immediate transaction. Routes finance/routes.js:25-30 invoke directly. A successful partial settlement followed by lost response, then identical retry can increase paid amount again while sufficient remaining balance exists. There is no stable operation UUID/payload hash/replay authorization ledger in this contract. Expected identical replay returns prior result, conflicting payload rejects, no second treasury/invoice/audit effect. Source inspection established missing mechanism; isolated behavioral reproduction pending. Smallest remediation: actor-scoped unique operation identity and canonical payload hash persisted transactionally with settlement, returning saved response after authorization. Acceptance: two identical partial calls, changed content, concurrent requests, response loss, cross-role replay; compare all ledger/invoice/audit effects.

BIZ-002 High / confirmed incomplete requirement: maintenance quantities not represented by representative visit contract. rep/service.js:304,321-340 accepts reasonIds and deduplicates IDs; persisted visit writes reason_ids_json at :937-942. No quantity payload, positive integer validation, per-reason quantity provenance or historical quantity default appears in this path. Thus selection of two or more of one countable reason cannot reach analytics/PPTX as requested. Expected per-visit multiple reasons with countability and quantities end to end. Smallest remediation: additive per-visit reason quantity records plus explicit historical provenance, UI/queue/server/admin correction/analytics/export changes. Acceptance: reject zero/fraction for new countable reason; two different quantities survive offline upload, approval, correction, analytics and PowerPoint. Existing reason selection is an implemented capability, not quantity completion.
